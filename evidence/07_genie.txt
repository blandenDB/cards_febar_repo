Q: What is the penetration rate by generation across all tiers?
assessment: GOOD 
Genie SQL:
SELECT
  `generation`,
  SUM(`qualified_targeted`) AS `qualified_targeted`,
  SUM(`new_cards`) AS `new_cards`,
  ROUND(try_divide(SUM(`new_cards`),SUM(`qualified_targeted`)), 4) AS `penetration_rate`
FROM `c360_bank`.`cards`.`v_cdo_summary`
WHERE `generation` IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY `generation`
HAVING SUM(`qualified_targeted`) > 0
ORDER BY `generation`

Q: Which 5 cities had the most new cards?
assessment: GOOD 
Genie SQL:
WITH `city_totals` AS (
  SELECT
    `city`,
    SUM(`new_cards`) AS `new_cards`
  FROM `c360_bank`.`cards`.`v_cdo_summary`
  WHERE `city` IS NOT NULL
  GROUP BY `city`
), `ranked_cities` AS (
  SELECT
    `city`,
    `new_cards`,
    RANK() OVER (ORDER BY `new_cards` DESC) AS `city_rank`
  FROM `city_totals`
)
SELECT
  `city`,
  `new_cards`
FROM `ranked_cities`
WHERE `city_rank` <= 5
ORDER BY `city_rank`, `city`

Question: Do rising grocery prices drive Gold card acceptance? Show monthly Gold penetration next to the 3-month change in grocery prices.
Genie SQL:
SELECT
  s.month,
  SUM(s.qualified_targeted) AS qualified_targeted,
  SUM(s.new_cards) AS new_cards,
  ROUND(try_divide(SUM(s.new_cards), SUM(s.qualified_targeted)), 4) AS gold_penetration_rate,
  MAX(p.food_at_home_3m_change) AS food_at_home_3m_change
FROM c360_bank.cards.v_cdo_summary s
LEFT JOIN c360_bank.cards.v_price_trends p
  ON p.month = s.month
 AND p.metro_area = s.metro_area
WHERE s.tier = 'Gold'
  AND s.month IS NOT NULL
  AND p.food_at_home_3m_change IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY s.month
ORDER BY s.month

- **2025-06:** Gold penetration was **0.0768** with grocery prices up just **0.0018**
- **2025-09:** Gold penetration was **0.0554** while grocery prices were up **0.0112**
- **2026-05:** Gold penetration was **0.0851** with grocery prices up **0.0047**
- **2026-06:** Gold penetration reached its high of **0.0894** while grocery prices were up **0.0113**

Across all **24 months**, Gold penetration ranged from **0.0476** to **0.0894** while the 3-month grocery price change ranged from **0.0008** to **0.0113**, with both high and low penetration appearing at both higher and lower grocery inflation levels.
